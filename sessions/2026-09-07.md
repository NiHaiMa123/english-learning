# Session — 2026-09-07

## Mode

- Scheduled normal lesson
- Full multimodal with Voice
- Approximate duration: ~20–25 minutes of active lesson/discussion, plus Voice configuration testing afterward

## Topic

Blender long-term-support / stability theme, used mainly as a listening calibration context.

## What happened

The initial listening item was too difficult. It was a natural technical explanation of roughly 80–100 words. The learner could not recover the gist and later reported that almost all words felt unfamiliar or could not be segmented reliably in speech.

Repeating the same content did not help enough. After the explanation was simplified and translated into Chinese, the learner could work with the core meaning: the version is more stable, has fewer bugs, and focuses less on new features.

This is important calibration evidence: current listening difficulty needs to be much lower than originally planned.

## New / practiced language

- `bug / bugs`
- `fewer bugs`
- `stability`
- `stability issues`
- `suggest fixing ...`
- `prioritize fixing ...`
- singular `version`

Contextual target sentences practiced:

- `I suggest fixing the stability issues first.`
- `Prioritize fixing the stability issues.`
- `The new version has fewer bugs.`

## Important errors / corrections

- `less bugs` -> `fewer bugs`
- `The new versions has few bugs` -> `The new version has fewer bugs`
- `suggest to fix` / malformed attempt -> `suggest fixing`
- `stablility` -> `stability`
- `prioritize` was difficult to retrieve/pronounce fluently in Voice

## Listening observations

- Natural technical paragraph at the original level was far above current usable listening level.
- Main difficulty appeared to be lexical recognition/segmentation as well as overall density, not only speed.
- When the learner said they did not know almost all the words, continuing in English created more confusion.
- Chinese explanation restored comprehension quickly.
- Future listening should begin with one short 5–10 word sentence at a time, mostly known words, with at most one new item.
- After one failed attempt, simplify or give Chinese support rather than repeatedly replaying the same dense sentence.

## Speaking observations

- Spontaneous speaking is currently much weaker than writing/recognition.
- The learner can produce short chunks and one short sentence after modeling.
- Longer output quickly causes retrieval breakdown and hesitation.
- Speaking should therefore move to the end of the lesson and stay at S0–S1 initially.
- Suitable next tasks: repeat a chunk, complete a frame, choose an expression, or produce one short sentence from Chinese.

## Pronunciation observations

- `prioritize` caused repeated hesitation in Voice and should be re-tested later in a short new sentence.
- Do not infer other pronunciation errors from transcript text alone.
- Chinese Voice accent itself affected comfort/comprehension; the learner switched Voice and found the new result only barely acceptable. Keep Chinese explanations concise and clear.

## Writing / Prompt observations

Writing is noticeably stronger than spontaneous speaking.

Successful outputs after correction:
- `I suggest fixing the stability issues first.`
- `Prioritize fixing the stability issues.`

The learner can absorb and reuse a concise Agent-style pattern after one focused correction.

## Successful production

- `fewer bugs`
- `I suggest fixing the stability issues first.`
- `prioritize fixing the stability issues`

These are not yet considered mastered because they required support and should be reviewed in new contexts.

## Learner feedback that changes the plan

- Put speaking at the end.
- Speaking foundation feels too weak; lower the difficulty substantially.
- Increase Chinese proportion, especially in Voice, because English-only instructions/explanations are hard to follow.
- Keep listening as the highest priority, but make it comprehensible rather than simply harder.
