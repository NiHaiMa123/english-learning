## New language introduced

### `verify` vs `validate`
Working distinction for current technical contexts:
- `verify` — check whether a specific fact/result is correct; e.g. `Verify that the fix resolves the reported issue.`
- `validate` — check whether an approach/result satisfies requirements or intended use; e.g. `Validate the approach against the requirements.`

Learner correctly chose `verify` for checking whether a fix resolves a reported issue and `validate` for checking an approach against requirements.

### `assumption` / `unverified assumption`
Useful chunks:
- `be based on an assumption`
- `an unverified assumption`

Learner first produced:
- `this result bases on an assumption that dosen't be verified`

Preferred:
- `This conclusion is based on an unverified assumption.`
